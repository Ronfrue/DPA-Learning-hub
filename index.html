Tips for a good answer

  • Say who holds the data and whose data it is.
  • Name the kind of data (health, contact details, CCTV, ID numbers).
  • Say what happened or what you plan to do, and when.
  • Mention any other country or outside company involved.

    The numbers people ask about most. Amounts are in Jamaican dollars.

    Current status. The OIC has temporarily paused data controller registration for system updates and has said no liability for unregistered processing will be imposed while the system is offline. All other duties, including breach reporting and the annual DPIA, still apply. Last checked 5 November 2026.
